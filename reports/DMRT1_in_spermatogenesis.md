# DMRT1 in spermatogenesis

## Executive Summary

DMRT1 is a conserved DM-domain transcription factor that acts sequentially with DMRT6 and DMRT7 to coordinate mammalian spermatogenesis, and is required for spermatogonial stem cell (SSC) maintenance, spermatogonial differentiation, and the mitosis/meiosis switch [[1]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026)[[2]](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1006293)[[3]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026). In humans, DMRT1 induction in PGC-like cells triggers germline commitment and suppresses pluripotency, establishing competence for gametogenesis [[4]](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-023-01224-7). DMRT1 also maintains testicular immune homeostasis via the SPRY1â€“NF-ÎºB axis, and its variants cause male infertility and impaired transcriptional activity [[5]](https://doi.org/10.24272/j.issn.2095-8137.2022.440)[[6]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fertnstert.2022.10.032).

## Detailed Answer

### 1. DMRT proteins act sequentially to coordinate mammalian spermatogenesis [[1]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026)

Zhang & Zarkower (2017) established that three Dmrt genes act in sequence to establish and maintain spermatogenesis in mice:

| Ref | Protein | Expression | Key Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[1]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026) | DMRT1 | All mitotic germ cells + Sertoli cells | Represses pluripotency genes; promotes mitotic arrest in embryonic germ cells; reactivates prospermatogonia perinatally; establishes/maintains SSCs; promotes spermatogonial differentiation; controls mitosis/meiosis switch |
| [[1]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026) | DMRT6 | A4, Intermediate, type B spermatogonia | Represses Dmrt1/Sohlh1/Sohlh2, coordinating orderly exit from mitotic program for correct meiotic timing |
| [[1]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026) | DMRT7 | Late primary spermatocytes | Choreographs histone-modification transition maintaining post-meiotic sex chromatin silencing |

Key evidence: in Dmrt1 mutants, **Stra8 and other meiosis-promoting factors are overexpressed in A1 spermatogonia**, causing premature meiotic entry that bypasses the spermatogonial proliferation program [[1]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026). During postnatal spermatogenesis, DMRT1 is expressed in all mitotic germ cells, DMRT6 in A4/Intermediate/type B spermatogonia, and DMRT7 in late primary spermatocytes [[1]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026).

### 2. DMRT1 is required for spermatogonial stem cell maintenance and replenishment [[2]](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1006293)[[3]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026)

Zhang, Oatley & Zarkower (2016, PLoS Genetics) showed DMRT1 supports SSC homeostasis in **two ways**: allowing SSCs to remain in the stem cell pool under normal conditions, and enabling progenitor cells to restore the stem cell pool after germ cell depletion (e.g., busulfan treatment) [[2]](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1006293)[[3]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026).

- In Dmrt1-mutant SSCs, **Plzf expression is greatly reduced**, leading to SSC loss and progressive spermatogenesis failure [[3]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026).
- In busulfan-treated Dmrt1-mutant (Ngn3+ spermatogonia) mice, surviving undifferentiated spermatogonia cannot replenish lost SSCs, and spermatogenesis fails to recover [[3]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2017.07.026).

### 3. DMRT1 regulates human germline commitment [[4]](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-023-01224-7)

Irie et al. (2023, Nature Cell Biology) â€?a study from the Surani laboratory â€?showed that **induction of DMRT1 in human PGC-like cells triggers germline commitment** while suppressing pluripotency genes, promoting the onset of gametogenesis [[4]](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-023-01224-7).

Mechanistic findings:
- DMRT1 + SOX17 induction in PGC-like cells promotes **epigenetic resetting** with global enrichment of 5-hydroxymethylcytosine and locus-specific loss of 5-methylcytosine at DMRT1 binding sites [[4]](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-023-01224-7).
- DMRT1 induces the later PGC programme â€?**DAZL, PIWIL1, PIWIL2** â€?and specific repeat elements (LTR12C, ALR/Alpha), hallmarks of germline commitment [[4]](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-023-01224-7).
- DMRT1 suppresses pluripotency genes during this transition [[4]](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-023-01224-7).

This provides the human-specific evidence linking DMRT1 directly to germline commitment and in vitro gametogenesis [[4]](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-023-01224-7).

### 4. DMRT1 maintains testicular immune homeostasis via SPRY1â€“NF-ÎºB [[5]](https://doi.org/10.24272/j.issn.2095-8137.2022.440)

Zhang MF et al. (2023, Zoological Research) used a **seminiferous-tubule-targeted Dmrt1 knockdown** (siRNA) mouse model (rather than knockout, which causes complete testicular failure) [[5]](https://doi.org/10.24272/j.issn.2095-8137.2022.440).

- **Methods** (from full text): histology, IHC/IF/electron microscopy (anti-DMRT1, anti-DDX4, anti-ZBTB16, anti-SYCP3); Western blot + Co-IP (NF-ÎºB p65/phospho-p65, SPRY1, cleaved Caspase3); RNA-seq (Si-NC vs Si-Dmrt1, 2 replicates); ChIP-seq of FLAG-Dmrt1 in primary Sertoli cells (5 kb window around TSS/TES) [[5]](https://doi.org/10.24272/j.issn.2095-8137.2022.440).
- **Conclusion**: Dmrt1 knockdown inhibits spermatogenesis and triggers broad seminiferous-tubule inflammation; the **DMRT1 â†?SPRY1 â†?NF-ÎºB** axis maintains testicular immune homeostasis, and its disruption causes spermatogenic failure and male infertility [[5]](https://doi.org/10.24272/j.issn.2095-8137.2022.440).

### 5. DMRT1 variants cause human male infertility [[6]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fertnstert.2022.10.032)

Emich et al. (2023, Fertility & Sterility) functionally assessed patient-derived DMRT1 missense variants using a **novel luciferase reporter assay** (CYP19A1 and Stra8 promoters as DMRT1 targets driving firefly luciferase in HEK293T cells) [[6]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fertnstert.2022.10.032).

- Multiple variants impair DMRT1 transcriptional activation of target promoters, supporting pathogenicity in isolated male infertility [[6]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fertnstert.2022.10.032).
- Variants classified per ACMG-AMP guidelines (likely benign / VUS / likely pathogenic) [[6]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fertnstert.2022.10.032).

## Limitations & Unverified Claims

- The DMRT1â†’SPRY1â†’NF-ÎºB axis (2023) is recent; its conservation in human testis remains to be validated [[5]](https://doi.org/10.24272/j.issn.2095-8137.2022.440).

- Functional assays in [[6]](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fertnstert.2022.10.032) used HEK293T cells; germline-context validation is still needed.
- Preprint [[9]](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.07.29.667536) (SMARCA5â€“DMRT1 pioneer complex) is not peer-reviewed.
